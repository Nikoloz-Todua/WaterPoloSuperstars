# Inspect Milestone 30B in Unity

Open the project in Unity 6000.6.7f1. Choose **Tools > Water Polo > Play Hub Entry** for the normal flow or **Tools > Water Polo > Play Olympic Match Arena** for a direct full match. Let the intro advance once before using Backspace or **Skip Intro** on later runs. Use **F6** to compare the live cameras and **F8** to cycle the existing inspection views. Stop Play before starting a different preview. Each command in **Tools > Water Polo > Verification > Milestone 30B** starts an isolated arena and restores Boot when Play stops. The previews stage positions and can park opposition.

| What to inspect | Menu or scenario and controls | What to look for | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Briefs and caps | **Play Olympic Match Arena**. Wait through Team Entrance and Formal Lineup. Inspect a Blue field player, White field player and both red-capped keepers. In an isolated close-up, use **Tools > Water Polo > Athletes > Play Athlete Preview**, then **Toggle Rebuilt Briefs in Preview**. Press **F3** to frame the cloth, **Left/Right** to orbit, **1/2/3/6** for tread/crawl/sprint/shot, and **F4** for walking. | Blue cloth is dark blue. White cloth reads pale on deck and cool gray-blue in water. Follow the waistband and both leg openings. At high leg flexion, look for exposed skin through fabric, flicker and seams separating. View the waist at low and high angles since water affects contrast. | [Lineup](Evidence/Milestone30B/Equipment-Lineup.png) · [Blue](Evidence/Milestone30B/Equipment-Blue-Field-Front.png) · [White](Evidence/Milestone30B/Equipment-White-Field-Front.png) |
| Palm over ball | **Verification > Milestone 30B > Possession and Throwing**. Release movement after the staged pickup. | The ball floats to the throwing side, with the back of the hand and spread fingers over its top. The hand underneath or the ball obscuring the face indicates a defect. | [Low hold](Evidence/Milestone30B/Throwing/Low-Hold.png) |
| Rear-loaded pose, quick and standard throws | In **Possession and Throwing**, hold **Space** (or **SHOOT**) to charge. Inspect from the side, then release. Restart the preview, enter forward crawl with possession and tap **Space**. | Look for the ball, forearm and elbow behind the throwing shoulder in the loaded frame. A quick action should gather, lift the head and torso, release from the hand once and follow through. A ball that leaves a head-down swimmer or dips to the relaxed hold during a charged release indicates a defect. | [Rear load](Evidence/Milestone30B/Throwing/Rear-Loaded.png) · [Shot sequences](Evidence/Milestone30B/Throwing/) |
| Turning, protected retreat and sprint drop | **Possession and Throwing** or a normal live match. Use **WASD/arrows** or the joystick. Turn through a circle by taking successive angled steps. Move opposite the athlete's current facing, then hold **Left Shift** or **SPRINT**. | The athlete keeps their head facing play while moving backwards upright. The ball stays in reach during the turn and drops once from the held point on sprint. The drop should stay near that point while the athlete retreats. Look for drifting attachment, a snap turn, crawl being played backwards, or immediate unwanted recapture. | [Turning frames](Evidence/Milestone30B/Throwing/) |
| Receiver switching and hand contact | **Passing and Switching**. Face the staged teammate and press **B** or **PASS**. Repeat and press **C** or **SWITCH** after the selection indicator transfers and before the catch. In a normal match, also observe a loose ball closer to a teammate than to the selected athlete. | On a targeted pass, the indicator and HUD move to the receiver while the ball is in flight. The receiver turns their head and reaches with one hand. A manual switch should remain in effect through the catch. Loose recovery should pick a reachable field player without rapid oscillation. Watch for a ball drawn into the chest, or a switch while White legally holds the ball. | [Passing sequences](Evidence/Milestone30B/Throwing/) |
| Prone feet, stroke effects and weighted turns | **Underwater Freestyle** and a normal above-water match. Swim with **WASD/arrows** or the joystick. Alternate normal and **Shift/SPRINT**. Release movement to tread. Try a sharp reversal while sprinting without the ball. Change quality with **Tools > Water Polo > Arena > Water Preview > Low / Medium / High**. | Toes should extend rearward with alternating hips and knees through the stroke. Look for ankle inversion or rigid feet during transitions. Above water, both hands should create a splash at their own entry and leave wake behind the swimmer. During the reversal the body and path need a brief turn, with short directional wash. Large circles without contact indicate a defect. | [Equipment side and underwater samples](Evidence/Milestone30B/) |
| Ordinary drop and skip | Choose **Ordinary Ball Drop** and watch the automatic drop. Choose **Skip Shot** for the staged launch or hold **Q** before **Space** in live play. On touch, choose **SKIP** before using **SHOOT**. | A drop makes one short splash, dips and settles without repeated jumps. The skip has forward spray and a low upward rebound while retaining forward travel. It loses energy. Steep or slow water entry settles instead of bouncing repeatedly. | [Water evidence folder](Evidence/Milestone30B/) |
| Field block and centre marking | Choose **Center Defense and Shot Block**. Let the nearby defender set up, then aim toward goal and release **Space**. Repeat in a full match using **Z** or **DEFENSE** to compare Press and Zone. | One relevant defender should lift one arm when the shot commits. Watch the centre back shade the passing lane without abandoning the opposing centre. After a block the athlete should resume tracking. Look for synchronized team-wide arm raises or a centre back drifting to every ball carrier. | See the verification status in [plan.md](../plan.md). |
| Keeper saves | Choose **Keeper Save Left**, **Keeper Save Right** or **Keeper Save High**. The preview takes the shot after settling its opening pose. In a full match, vary the lane and compare a catch, deflection and goal. | The keeper should drive upward and lean toward the incoming ball. Watch the near hand extend, the other arm balance, the water displacement, and the return to eggbeater. A legitimate miss can occur. Look for a ball that stops far from the hand or a static T-pose. | See the verification status in [plan.md](../plan.md). |
| Walk, officials and replay | Start a full arena run. Watch the feet of both teams and both officials through the entrance. After a goal watch both replay angles, then **Enter** to advance or **Backspace / Skip All** to exit. | The stance leg should roll naturally through heel contact and toe-off. Officials should look toward the pool while walking and point in the awarded direction. In replay, inspect the kit and cap numbers, ball release, defenders, water and scoreboard through both angles. A replay should leave the score and ownership alone. | [Walkout sequence](Evidence/Milestone30B/Equipment-Walkout-A.png) · [Walkout second frame](Evidence/Milestone30B/Equipment-Walkout-B.png) |

After direct arena or Athlete Preview testing, stop in Edit Mode and choose **Tools > Water Polo > Recovery > Use Boot as Editor Play Entry**. **Tools > Water Polo > Arena > Restore Previous Water Presentation**, **Restore Previous Throwing**, **Restore Milestone 29 Deck Presentation**, and **Tools > Water Polo > Athletes > Restore Previous Competition Briefs** retain the earlier options. Their Apply counterparts restore the saved Milestone 30 scene features. The M30B source edits are listed in [the exact inventory](MILESTONE_30B_FILES.txt).
