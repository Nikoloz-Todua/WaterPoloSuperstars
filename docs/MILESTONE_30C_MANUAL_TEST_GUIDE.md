# Inspect Milestone 30C in Unity

Use Unity **6000.6.7f1**. Stop Play between cases. The paths below start at **Tools > Water Polo**. In contact and aquatic tests wait for the staged camera and athletes. Test Lab uses the existing arena and isolated Play state.

## Start here

| Test and exact menu | Reproduction | Expected and defect signs | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Test Lab > Player Contact and Defense > Head-On Centre Players** | Hold **W/Up Arrow** as the White centre back approaches. Release, then move sideways and approach again. | Both torsos resist, tread at contact, and separate when you disengage. Crossing through the other centre, sudden escape impulses or continuing shoulder-to-shoulder jitter indicate a defect. | [Top and close side motion](Evidence/Milestone30C/Contact-Sequence.jpg) |
| **Test Lab > Player Contact and Defense > Shoulder Slide** | Hold **W/Up Arrow**. Repeat with slight **A/D** or joystick input to steer along the shoulder. | A glancing approach slides toward the open lane. Bodies passing through each other, the stationary athlete being launched, or getting stuck when steering out indicates a defect. | [Contact and measurements](PLAYER_CONTACT_AND_CONTROL_AUTHORITY.md) |
| **Test Lab > Player Contact and Defense > Sprint into Stationary Defender** | Hold **W/Up Arrow + Left Shift**. Keep sprint pressed briefly on contact, then turn away. | The athlete stops their inward path and can leave the contact. A sprint through the defender or violent recoil indicates a defect. | [Contact and measurements](PLAYER_CONTACT_AND_CONTROL_AUTHORITY.md) |
| **Test Lab > Player Contact and Defense > Contested Loose Ball** | Hold **W/Up Arrow**. Use movement alone and leave **E** and **COLLECT** untouched. | One athlete gathers the reachable ball and the other loses the contest. A slowly bobbing ball that repeatedly escapes both athletes at hand reach indicates a defect. | [Pickup conditions and measured cases](PLAYER_CONTACT_AND_CONTROL_AUTHORITY.md) |
| **Test Lab > Ball Control and Player Switching > Possession and Throwing** | Hold the ball with no command, turn with **WASD/arrows**, then pause/resume with **Esc**. Repeat and press **C** to switch away. Allow the existing protection window to expire. Then press **B**, or hold and release **Space**, deliberately. | Movement, rotation, UI and selection leave the ball held. An off-control Blue carrier holds and can become selected after protection. A deliberate command produces one throw. A free ball or throw animation during an unrelated action indicates a defect. | [Control and provenance results](PLAYER_CONTACT_AND_CONTROL_AUTHORITY.md) |
| **Test Lab > Swimming and Floating > Underwater Freestyle** | Hold camera-relative forward, or use the joystick in the athlete's visible direction of travel. Chest and front of the legs read as one prone body. Both ankles extend rearward and the feet roll with the body. Looking like backstroke legs below a prone torso or losing the alternating kick indicates a defect. | [Before and after](Evidence/Milestone30C/Prone-Comparison.jpg) · [Sprint, leading and eggbeater](Evidence/Milestone30C/Prone-Variants.jpg) |
| **Test Lab > Ceremony and Walking > Arena Walkout and Pennants** | Advance the overview with **Enter** or tap, then let walkout, lineup and exchange run at full speed. Repeat and wait briefly at each stage. | Observe forward swing, support, knee direction, sole angle and toe-off. In the lineup and at the actual exchange, watch both captains' heels and toes on the deck. Look for skating, backward bends, lifted soles or feet cutting through the floor. | [Deck motion sequence](Evidence/Milestone30C/Deck-Sequence.jpg) |

## Existing behaviors and open acceptance items

| Feature | Repeatable inspection and what to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Receiving and the palm gap** | In **Test Lab > Shooting and Passing > Passing and Switching**, press **B** toward the staged receiver. Watch the uphill and downhill approaches, then press **C** once during the next pass. The intended target should be selected in flight unless the manual choice is protected. The first visible palm contact, long pass to a swimmer facing away and realistic receiving turn remain open. The current menu stages a short aligned pass. [30B hand-contact sequences](Evidence/Milestone30B/Passing-and-Catching.jpg) |
| **Aim and held rotation** | In **Test Lab > Ball Control and Player Switching > Possession and Throwing**, make gradual **WASD/arrow** angle changes, face a target, and press **B**. Move opposite the visible heading to retreat, and add **Shift** deliberately to use the existing voluntary drop. Aim indicator and independent directional carrying are deferred. [30B turning sequence](Evidence/Milestone30B/Held-Ball-Turning.jpg) |
| **Full-power and quick shots** | In **Possession and Throwing**, hold **Space** for at least 0.7 s and release, then restart and make a short tap after starting forward movement. Check the throwing hand and release. Extra elevation, lean variations and shooting splash remain deferred. [Inherited shot sequence](Evidence/Milestone30B/Shooting-Sequences.jpg) |
| **Goalkeeper catches and saves** | Choose **Test Lab > Goalkeeper and Penalties > Keeper Save Left**, **Keeper Save Right**, or **Keeper Save High**. The shot starts automatically. Inspect the hand, body rise and return to tread. An isolated routine-catch or neutral tracking scenario remains pending. [Inherited directional sequence](Evidence/Milestone30B/Keeper-Save-Sequence.jpg) |
| **Foul restart and legal movement** | The focused `Milestone30CControlVerification.RestartAndDryMiss` runner awards a Blue ordinary foul and waits past the former auto-release window. In the ready phase, existing **B/PASS** is the accepted pass command. New palm-over-ball restart pose and staged independent legal repositioning remain deferred. The automated runner exercises restart authority and its stopped clock. [Restart status](RECEIVING_AIMING_AND_MATCH_CONTINUITY.md) |
| **Goals and near misses** | The new goal reactions and delayed out-of-play flight are deferred. Existing scoring replays and restarts remain available through **Play > Play Match**. The independent 30C celebration, conceding-team, near-miss and sideline presentation scenarios remain pending. |
| **Idle disturbances and baseline water** | Use **Test Lab > Swimming and Floating > Freestyle and Floating**, release all movement and watch the wrists above the water. For a repeatable ball impact, use **Test Lab > Arena and Water > Ordinary Ball Drop**. The idle effect expansion is deferred. [Inherited water events](Evidence/Milestone30B/Hand-Entries-and-Wakes.jpg) |
| **Swimwear** | In **Test Lab > Athlete and Equipment > Play Athlete Preview**, press **F3** and orbit with **Left/Right**. Compare the 30C **Arena Walkout and Pennants** lineup with **Underwater Freestyle** and **Underwater Ball Leading**. Check the existing briefs for hip and leg opening seams and Blue/White contrast. For red-capped keepers watch the formal lineup or the directional keeper labs. Replay and high-shot garment validation remain pending. [30C deck](Evidence/Milestone30C/Deck-Sequence.jpg) · [30C crawl](Evidence/Milestone30C/Prone-Variants.jpg) · [30B live kit inventory](Evidence/Milestone30B/Production-Kit-Inventory.tsv) |

Use **Tools > Water Polo > Recovery > Use Boot as Editor Play Entry** after direct tests if the Play entry did not return automatically. For the full local flow, launch **Tools > Water Polo > Play > Play Full Game**.
