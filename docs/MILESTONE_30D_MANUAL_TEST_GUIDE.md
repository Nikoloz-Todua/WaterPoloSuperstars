# Inspect Milestone 30D in Unity

Use Unity **6000.6.7f1**. All paths start at **Tools > Water Polo > Test Lab**. Launch from a saved scene in Edit Mode. Stop Play between scenarios and wait for the staged camera.

| Scenario | Controls and expected result | What indicates a defect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Swimming and Floating > Underwater Freestyle** | Move with **WASD**, arrows or the joystick. Keep moving for several complete cycles. Hold **Left Shift** or **SPRINT** to compare sprint. Release movement to return to eggbeater. **Freestyle and Floating** gives a side view. | Knees and calves should read as one prone chain from the hips. The hips lead smaller alternating kicks and the ankles trail the shin. Backstroke-like legs, a sole rolling independently of the ankle, or a snapped transition still need attention. |
| **Swimming and Floating > Underwater Ball Leading** | Sustain camera-relative forward input until the ball moves ahead of the head. Alternate normal and sprint input. | The freestyle chain should retain its anatomical orientation while the ball leads. The previous holding and eggbeater should return naturally after slowing. |
| **Shooting and Passing > Long Pass to Receiver Facing Away** | The far Blue receiver starts looking away. Press **B** or tap **PASS**. For a separate trial press **C** during flight to retain your manual selection, or steer the receiver away from the ball. | The receiver turns toward incoming flight before contact, opens a reachable palm and absorbs the ball. A catch behind the body, ball homing or snapping, or forced selection after a manual choice indicates a defect. Moving away can result in a legitimate miss. |
| **Ball Control and Player Switching > Possession and Throwing** | Hold **Left Alt + WASD/arrows** to rotate in place. Aim left, right, diagonally, forward and back. Release Alt and press **B**. On touch, drag **PASS** in the intended direction, then release for a dry pass. A stationary hold still requests a wet pass. Use **F6** in the broadcast view to compare cameras. | The small green arrow indicates the same heading used for the pass cone or open-water direction. Sharp air-ball curves, an arrow selecting a player far outside the cone, or movement when aiming in place indicate a defect. |
| **Ball Control and Player Switching > Possession and Throwing** | Take a brief step sideways, turn with Alt or a PASS drag, and retreat against the facing direction. While retreating, use a lateral PASS drag. Then sustain forward movement until the ball leads. For the pre-existing voluntary drop, deliberately sprint backward. | Short repositioning keeps a hand on the ball. Retreat remains upright, and sustained forward travel progressively leads the ball. A pass or shot from turning alone, a head intersection, or an ordinary turn that drops the ball indicates a defect. |
| **Shooting and Passing > Full Power Shooting Elevation** | Hold **Space** for at least 0.7 s, then release. Use **SHOOT** the same way on touch. Watch the rear load, leg press, torso rise, splash, grip release and return to water. | The pelvis and shoulders should rise substantially. Part of the upper brief can clear the surface. One ball leaves the hand on command. A second ball, a distant release point, or an athlete held at the peak indicates a defect. |
| **Shooting and Passing > Quick Shooting Elevation** | Tap **Space** or **SHOOT** briefly while holding the ball. | The rise and load are shorter and lower than a full charge. Look for a compact forward release and balanced recovery. |
| **Referees and Match Rules > Ordinary Foul Repositioning** | Wait for Free Throw Ready. Leave **B**, **L**, **PASS** and **LOB** untouched while watching both teams move. Use **B/PASS** when ready. | The ball remains under the taker's palm on the mark and the clocks wait. Attacking teammates swim to outlets and defenders adjust without entering the protected circle. A spontaneous throw, moving clocks before release, frozen off-ball athletes or a defender swimming through the taker indicates a defect. |

**Underwater Sprint Freestyle** and **Directional Aim and Held Rotation** are dedicated aliases under the same Swim and Ball Control folders. The aim arrow gives the requested horizontal direction. Targeted passes can deviate within the existing assist cone as they travel to a reachable hand. Shots share that horizontal intent while retaining their existing vertical targeting and charge.
