# Milestone 30E manual Test Lab guide

Use Unity **6000.6.7f1**. Start from saved Edit Mode and wait for the staged view. Stop Play between trials to reset the positions and possession.

## Protected possession

Open **Tools > Water Polo > Test Lab > Ball Control and Player Switching > Directional Aim and Held Rotation**.

1. Leave the controls alone. Look for upright eggbeater, a partly floating ball and the palm over it.
2. Use **Left Alt + WASD or arrows** to face the corresponding screen direction in place. Try left, right, the opposite direction and four successive quarter turns. The shoulders, torso, head, rear-loaded arm, ball and chevron should all come around together.
3. Release Alt and briefly move along the chevron, then across it. The hand should lift the ball behind the shoulder before purposeful travel. The athlete should stay upright and slow during the short adjustment. Release movement and look for the ball to lower back to the surface.
4. Move opposite the chevron to retreat. While holding the same retreat direction, press **numpad 4** or **6** to aim to screen left or right, or **8** or **2** for up or down. Adjacent aim keys combine diagonally. You can intentionally pass with **B** while steering this way. Release movement before choosing a new forward or backward heading.
5. Reorient, then hold movement along the chevron for about half a second. Watch the ball move ahead of the head and both arms enter freestyle. **Left Shift + forward** should get to sprint crawl sooner. With the ball raised during retreat, **Left Shift + backward** should deliberately drop it once and briefly prevent the former holder from reclaiming it.

A flat palm pushing the ball while the athlete swims, a shoulder or hand staying in the old direction as the player turns, a ball cutting across the face, an uncommanded throw, or repeated dribble-to-hold switching indicates a defect.

## Directional passes

Open **Tools > Water Polo > Test Lab > Shooting and Passing > Directional Passing**. Outlets start on both sides of the holder.

- Aim toward the left outlet with **Left Alt + Left Arrow** and press **B**. Restart the scenario and repeat on the right. You can press **B** during the turn. The pass should leave the animated hand after the body has aligned with the selected lane.
- Watch the pool-level marker at the selection ring. It should be a short, two-stroke **>** rotating with the athlete. A shaft, a marker pointing ahead of the torso turn, or a pass bending in flight indicates a defect. Aim into open water for a pass along the selected heading.
- Press **F6** to switch between Lengthwise and Sideline Broadcast. Aim toward a visible outlet using the new screen direction. **F8** cycles the separate inspection views. 
- Keyboard **Q+B** retains the wet pass. **Space** retains shot charge and release. **Q+Space** and **R+Space** retain the existing skip and high shot modifiers.

## Mobile controls

Use the joystick for camera-relative movement. Pull opposite the chevron to retreat. While holding the joystick, drag **PASS** with a second touch to turn toward the drag. Lift that touch to throw a dry pass. A **PASS** tap uses the accepted heading and a stationary hold of at least 0.32 s requests a wet pass. **SPRINT** and **SHOOT** retain their normal hold behavior. Check simultaneous touches and visibility on your phone.
