All five Meshy originals are under `SourceAssets/Athletes/WPS_Swimwear_001/Original/`, including the FBX, albedo, normal, roughness and metallic maps. The original Desktop path was absent. The 29B fitting script shrank the supplier shell toward a nearest surface without matching its three opening loops to the matching body cut. It transferred skin weights but carried the misregistered openings into the posed mesh. Its 2,002-triangle shell missed up to 24.5% of the sampled coverage area, and Blender close-ups showed gaps at the waist and pinching at the rear.

`Tools/Art/RebuildCompetitionBriefs.py` uses the production body's actual opening loops and the matched body-derived fabric panel. It registers 109 waist vertices and 78 and 73 vertices on the leg openings one to one. It copies exact paired skin positions and weights on the openings and resamples the panel along sewn-band distances. The raised waistband and both leg bindings are part of one skinned surface. A bounded interior relaxation changes how the fabric spans the body around the shaped openings. The existing body mask, Avatar and skin remain in use. The candidate uses the same palette-compatible periodic wrap, existing supplier-reprojected neutral fabric and tangent maps, and a vertex mask for waist, leg binding and side accent. The editable Blender material shows Blue, White and Red variants from the same mesh. The isolated Unity preview uses the current team material so its comparison isolates the geometric change.
